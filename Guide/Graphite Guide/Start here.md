# Welcome to the Graphite Guide

This guide is itself a vault: a folder of notes, pictures and PDFs that Graphite has opened, the same kind of folder you will keep your own notes in. Everything here works. Tap a link, tick a box, write on a slide, change a recipe. This is your own copy, so nothing you change here can break anything, and you can always ask the Guide for a fresh one.

> [!question] Where are you coming from?
> - **Goodnotes or Notability**, or you have never kept notes as files: start with [[What a vault is]]. It explains, in plain words, where your notes live and why that is good for you.
> - **Notion**: start with [[Moving in from Notion]]. Your pages come over with their covers, icons, columns and databases.
> - **Obsidian**: start with [[What Graphite opens]]. Your vault works as it is, and [[What Graphite adds]] shows the new parts.
> - **New to both**: read [[What a vault is]], then try the examples in [[Everyday examples]].

## How to read this guide

Most notes show a piece of syntax in a grey box, then what it looks like. The grey box is what you would type; below it is the result, drawn by Graphite. To see the plain text of any note, switch it to source mode: open the note's **More** menu (…) › **View** › **Source Mode**, and **Live Preview** to come back.

> [!tip] Reading or writing
> Graphite opens notes ready for writing. To read without changing anything by accident, tap the **pencil** button at the top right of the note (on the Mac, click it or press ⌘E).

The short how-to topics are in the app too: in Settings › General › **Guide** and on the welcome screen, and on the Mac also in Help › **Graphite Guide**. Each topic there has a button that opens the matching note here.
