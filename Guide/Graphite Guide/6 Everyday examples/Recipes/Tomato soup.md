# Tomato soup

![[Tomato soup.png|360]]

A thick, sweet soup of roasted tomatoes, the way it was served at [[Blue Tile Tasca]] on the [[Lisbon weekend]].

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 kg ripe tomatoes
- [ ] 2 red onions
- [ ] 3 cloves of garlic
- [ ] 2 tablespoons olive oil
- [ ] 500 ml vegetable stock
- [ ] A handful of basil
- [ ] Cream, to serve

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Halve the tomatoes, quarter the onions, and roast them with the garlic and oil for 30 minutes.
3. Blend with the hot stock until smooth. Season.
4. Serve with a spoon of cream and torn basil.

> [!tip] Make it a meal
> Serve it with bread and finish with [[Lemon cake]].
