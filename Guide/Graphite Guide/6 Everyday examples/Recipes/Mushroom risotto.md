# Mushroom risotto

![[Mushroom risotto.png|360]]

Inspired by the one at [[Fig Tree Kitchen]].

## Ingredients

- [ ] 250 g risotto rice[^rice]
- [ ] 300 g mushrooms
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 1 litre hot stock
- [ ] 50 g butter and some parmesan

[^rice]: Arborio or Carnaroli. See [[Footnotes and comments]] for how this footnote is written.

## Steps

1. Soften the onion in butter, add the rice and stir for a minute.
2. Add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for about 18 minutes.
3. Fry the mushrooms hard in another pan and fold them in.
4. Off the heat, beat in the rest of the butter and the parmesan.
