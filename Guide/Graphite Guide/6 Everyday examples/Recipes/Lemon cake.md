# Lemon cake

![[Lemon cake.png|360]]

A plain loaf with a sharp lemon syrup. Good after [[Tomato soup]].

## Ingredients

- [ ] 175 g butter, soft
- [ ] 175 g sugar
- [ ] 3 eggs
- [ ] 175 g self-raising flour
- [ ] 2 lemons, zest and juice
- [ ] 100 g icing sugar

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a loaf tin.
2. Beat the butter and sugar, then the eggs one at a time, then the flour and zest.
3. Bake for 45 minutes.
4. Mix the lemon juice with the icing sugar and pour it over the warm cake.

> [!question]- How do I know it is done?
> A skewer pushed into the middle comes out clean.
