# Recipes

A recipe collection: one note per dish, each with a picture and a few properties (how long, how many it serves, what kind of dish), and a base that shows them as a gallery.

![[Recipes.base]]

## What each recipe has

- **Properties** at the top: `minutes`, `servings`, `course`, `cover`, and the tag `recipe`.
- **Ingredients** as tasks, so the list doubles as a shopping list.
- **Steps** as a numbered list.
- **Links** to the restaurant where the idea came from, and to other recipes.

New recipes start from the [[Recipe]] template: **Insert** › **Template…** › *Recipe*.

> [!example] Share this folder
