# Pancakes

![[Pancakes.png|360]]

Sunday pancakes, thick and soft.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 200 g flour
- [ ] 2 teaspoons baking powder
- [ ] 1 tablespoon sugar
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 250 ml milk
- [ ] Butter for the pan
- [ ] Blueberries and syrup

## Steps

1. Whisk the dry things together, then the egg and milk.
2. Rest the batter for 10 minutes.
3. Cook spoonfuls in a buttered pan until bubbles show, then turn.

| Batch | Flour | Milk | Pancakes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| For two | 200 g | 250 ml | 8 |
| For four | 400 g | 500 ml | 16 |
