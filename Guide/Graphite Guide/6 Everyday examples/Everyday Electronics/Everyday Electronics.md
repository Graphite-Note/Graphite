## How a course can work in Graphite

1. Put the slides in the course's folder (**New** › **Import**, or the Files app).
2. Make one note per lecture from the [[Lecture]] template.
3. Embed the slide you are talking about in the note, at the height you like, and write beside it.
4. Write on the slides themselves during the lecture, with the [[Pencil tools]], and [[Recording a lecture|record the lecture]] if you are allowed.
5. Keep handwritten working in a [[Notebooks and paper|notebook]].
