# Formula sheet

| What | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Sampling without aliasing | $f_s > 2 f_{\max}$ |
| Period and frequency | $T = 1 / f$ |
| Sine wave | $x(t) = A \sin(2 \pi f t + \varphi)$ |
| Average of two samples | $y[n] = \tfrac{1}{2}(x[n] + x[n-1])$ |
| Echo | $y[n] = x[n] + a\, x[n - D]$ |
| Decibels | $L = 20 \log_{10}(A / A_0)$ |
