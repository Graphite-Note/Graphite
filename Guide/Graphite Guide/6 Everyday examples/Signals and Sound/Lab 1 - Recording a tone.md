# Lab 1: Recording a tone

Record a whistled note, then find its frequency from the samples alone.

## Steps

- [ ] Record two seconds of a steady whistle at 48 kHz
- [ ] Plot the first 10 ms: count the waves to estimate the frequency
- [ ] Plot the spectrum and read the tallest line
- [ ] Sample the same recording again at 8 kHz and listen: what changed?

> [!note] 📝
> Hand in the two plots and three sentences on what you heard. Theory: [[Week 1 - Sampling]].
