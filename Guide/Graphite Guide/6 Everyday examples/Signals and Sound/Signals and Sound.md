## ~={#D9730D}The one formula to remember=~

A signal must be sampled more than twice as fast as its highest frequency, or it comes back as a different, lower sound:

$$
f_s > 2 f_{\max}
$$

CD audio samples at $f_s = 44.1\ \text{kHz}$, so it keeps everything up to about $22\ \text{kHz}$, a little above what anyone hears.
