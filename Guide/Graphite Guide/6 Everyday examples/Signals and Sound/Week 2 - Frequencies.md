# Week 2: Frequencies

A note played on a flute and the same note on a violin have the same pitch but sound different. The difference is in their **harmonics**: every steady sound is a sum of sine waves at whole multiples of one frequency.

$$
x(t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n \sin\left(2 \pi n f_0 t + \varphi_n\right)
$$

| Instrument | Strong harmonics | Sounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flute | the first, little else | soft, pure |
| Clarinet | the odd ones: 1, 3, 5 | hollow |
| Violin | many, slowly weaker | bright |

> [!example]- Try it
> Whistle into the recorder of [[Lab 1 - Recording a tone]] and look at the spectrum: one tall line. Hum instead, and a row of lines appears.
