## ~={#337EA9}How much soil?=~

A round pot of radius $r$ filled to a height $h$ holds

$$
V = \pi r^2 h
$$

so a pot 30 cm across filled 25 cm deep takes $\pi \times 15^2 \times 25 \approx 17\,700\ \text{cm}^3$, about 18 litres. More in [[Soil and pots]].
