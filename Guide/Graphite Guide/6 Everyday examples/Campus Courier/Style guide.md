# Style guide

| Write | Not |
| --- | --- |
| 3 December | Dec. 3rd |
| 14:30 | 2.30 pm |
| the student council | the SC |
| 1,200 copies | 1200 copies |

- Headlines in sentence case, eight words at most.
- Numbers from one to nine in words, 10 and above in figures.
- ==Quote people exactly==, and say who they are the first time they appear.
