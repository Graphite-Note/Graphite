# Pitching a story

A pitch is three lines, said at the Monday meeting or added to the board in [[Campus Courier]]:

1. What happened, or will happen.
2. Why a student would want to read it.
3. Who you will talk to, and what picture it needs.

> [!example]- A pitch that worked
> The library keeps two floors open until midnight during exams. Students who study late will want to know which ones. I will talk to the librarian and two students on the night shift; a photo of the empty reading room at 23:00.

Then add a note in `Stories` with the stage **1 Pitched**. The numbers keep the board's columns in the order a story goes through: pitched, writing, editing, ready.
