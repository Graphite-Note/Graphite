## ~={#448361}How many sheets to print?=~

Each sheet folds into four pages, so an issue needs

$$
\text{sheets} = \frac{\text{pages}}{4} \times \text{copies}
$$

Sixteen pages and 1,200 copies take $4 \times 1\,200 = 4\,800$ sheets: ten boxes from the print shop.
