# Welcome aboard

Glad you're here. Your first month:

1. Come to a Tuesday meeting and say which division interests you.
2. Read [[Safety on the water]] and [[Workshop rules]].
3. Spend an evening with the division you chose. Nobody expects you to know anything yet.
