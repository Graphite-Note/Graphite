# Electrical

Six solar panels charge a 48 V battery that drives one motor. Nothing on the boat may be switched on before the battery box is closed.

| Part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Panels | 6 × 160 W |
| Battery | 48 V, 40 Ah |
| Motor | 2 kW at full power |

With full sun on all six panels, the boat gets $6 \times 160\ \text{W} = 960\ \text{W}$, about half of what the motor uses flat out.

> [!warning] ⚠️
> Two people at every battery job, and read [[Safety on the water]] first.
