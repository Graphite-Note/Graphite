## ~={#D44C47}Race day, 2027-06-12=~

The boat must last the two-hour endurance race on what its panels and battery give it:

$$
E = U \cdot Q = 48\ \text{V} \times 40\ \text{Ah} \approx 1.9\ \text{kWh}
$$

- [x] Hull painted and weighed
- [x] Panels mounted and tested in the sun
- [ ] Battery box sealed and checked by [[Electrical]]
- [ ] Telemetry sending speed and charge to the shore
- [ ] Trailer booked for the trip to the lake
