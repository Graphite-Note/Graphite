# Restaurants

Favourite places to eat, each a note with its place on the map, a rating, and what to order. They are made up, and so are their names; the places on the map are in Lisbon, for the [[Lisbon weekend]].

![[Restaurants.base#Map]]

![[Restaurants.base#By rating]]

## What each restaurant has

- `coordinates`: where it is, as two numbers (latitude and longitude). Any map app shows them for a place you pick.
- `cuisine`, `rating`, `visited` and `price`.
- What to order, and a link to the [[Recipes|recipe]] it inspired, if any.
