# Reading list

Books to read, being read and finished, each a note with a cover, an author, a status and your thoughts. The books and their authors are made up for this guide.

![[Reading list.base#Covers]]

## Three views of one base

The base [[Reading list.base]] shows the same notes as **Covers** (cards with the cover image), **By status** (a table grouped by `status`) and **Next to read** (a list of the books not started yet). Switch between them with the view's name at the top.
