# Backups

Your notes are files, so they are backed up the way files are. Choose at least one of these.

## A vault in iCloud Drive

A vault in iCloud Drive is copied to Apple's servers and to your other devices. That protects you from a lost or broken iPad. It does **not** protect you from deleting a note by mistake: a deletion syncs too. For that, see the next two sections.

## Graphite's own safety nets

- **Undo.** **Undo** and **Redo** are at the top of a note on iPad, above the keyboard on iPhone, and in Edit on the Mac (⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z). On iPad, touch and hold **Undo** for **Move Through Changes…**.
- **File Recovery.** Graphite keeps snapshots of your notes on the device. Open a note's **More** menu › **File Recovery…**, pick a time, and choose **Restore This Version**. Settings › File Recovery sets how often snapshots are taken and how long they are kept.
- **Deleted files** go to the system trash, where you can still bring them back. Settings › Files and Links › **Deleted Files** can use Obsidian's `.trash` folder in the vault instead.

## A copy somewhere else

Every so often, copy the vault's folder somewhere else:

- **On the Mac**, Time Machine backs up a vault on the Mac's disk or in iCloud Drive if it is downloaded. Or drag the folder to an external drive.
- **On iPad and iPhone**, in the Files app touch and hold the vault's folder, choose **Compress**, and save the ZIP somewhere else, such as another cloud drive.

> [!tip] A backup you can read anywhere
> A backup of a vault is useful even without Graphite: the notes are text, the notebooks are PDFs. Any computer can open them.
