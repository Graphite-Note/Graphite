# Moving in from Goodnotes and Notability

Your notebooks come with you. Graphite reads Goodnotes notebooks (`.goodnotes`) and Notability notes (`.note`) and turns each one into an ordinary PDF that keeps:

- every page's paper and page size,
- your handwriting, which stays editable with Apple Pencil,
- pictures, typed text boxes and sticky notes,
- the outline and bookmarks,
- Notability's recordings, as audio files next to the notebook.

The result opens in any PDF reader, and Graphite keeps editing it.

## Step 1: export from the old app

- **Goodnotes**: open the notebook (or select several, or a folder), choose **Share** › **Export**, and pick the **Goodnotes** format. A folder or a whole library exports as a ZIP.
- **Notability**: choose the note (or several), **Share**, and pick **Note** as the format.

## Step 2: bring them in

There are three ways, and they all do the same thing:

1. **Straight from the share sheet.** In the old app's share sheet, choose **Graphite**. The notebook is converted and lands in the vault that is open.
2. **From the Files app.** Save the export to Files first. Then in Graphite choose **New** › **Import** (on the Mac, File › **Import…**) and pick the notebooks, a whole folder of them, or a ZIP.
3. **By dragging.** Drag the files onto Graphite's file list.

Graphite shows the import's progress and, at the end, a short report of each notebook: how many pages, and anything it could not bring across.

> [!tip] A whole library at once
> Export your whole Goodnotes library as one ZIP and import that. Graphite unpacks it and keeps its folders, so your subjects stay in their folders.

> [!info] Turn it off to copy as is
> The conversion is a feature you can switch off in Settings › Core Plugins › **Goodnotes and Notability Import**. Off, the files are copied without being converted.

## What changes in your habits

| In Goodnotes or Notability | In Graphite |
| --- | --- |
| The library of notebooks | The sidebar's **Library** view, with page thumbnails, or the **List** view (see [[Library and list]]) |
| A new notebook | **New** › **Notebook** (see [[Notebooks and paper]]) |
| Writing on slides | Open the PDF and tap the **pencil** button (see [[Writing on slides]]) |
| Typed notes beside handwriting | A Markdown note with drawings inside it (see [[Drawings in notes]]) |
| Recording a lecture | The **microphone** button (see [[Recording a lecture]]) |
| Search | Search reads every note and the text boxes on PDF pages; inside one open PDF, **Find** reads its printed text. Handwriting is not searched yet |
