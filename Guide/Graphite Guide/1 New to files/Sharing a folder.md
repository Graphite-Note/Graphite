# Sharing a folder

You do not share a vault through Graphite; you share it the way you share any folder. Because a vault is a folder of ordinary files, you can share **one folder of it**, not the whole thing.

> [!example] Two ideas
> - A *Recipes* folder you share with your family, so everyone adds the dishes they cook. Look at [[Recipes]] for what it could hold.
> - A *Physics 101* folder you share with two classmates, with the slides, the lecture notes and everybody's solutions.

## With iCloud Drive

1. Keep the vault in iCloud Drive (see [[Where a vault can live]]).
2. In the **Files** app (or the Finder on the Mac), touch and hold the folder you want to share and choose **Share** › **Collaborate** (on the Mac, **Share** › **Share Folder**).
3. Invite people, and choose whether they can make changes or only look.
4. Each of them opens the shared folder in Graphite with **Open Folder as Vault**. It becomes a small vault of its own on their side.

## With another drive

Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox and others share folders from their own app or website. Once the folder is on someone's device through that drive's app, they open it with **Open Folder as Vault**.

## What to keep in mind

- **Links work inside the shared folder.** A link from a shared recipe to another shared recipe works for everyone. A link to a note outside the shared folder works only for you.
- **Pictures go with the notes.** Put a shared folder's pictures in that folder (or a folder inside it), so the people you share with see them too.
- **Edit different notes at the same time.** Two people changing the same note at the same moment can both win; Graphite shows both versions instead of losing one.
