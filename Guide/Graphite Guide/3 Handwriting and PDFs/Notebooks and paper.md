# Notebooks and paper

A notebook is a PDF whose pages carry paper: blank, dotted, squared, ruled, Cornell, engineering, isometric, music and more. It opens in any PDF reader, with your writing on it.

## Make one

1. Choose **New** › **Notebook** (in the library or at the top of the sidebar).
2. Name it, and pick its **Size** (A4, Letter, A5 and more, upright or sideways), its **Color** and the number of **Pages**.
3. Pick a paper from **Paper Templates**. **My Templates** holds papers you made from a PDF page of your own.
4. Tap the checkmark to make it, then write.

From inside a note, **Insert** › **New Notebook…** makes one and embeds it without leaving the note.

## Pages

- **Show Pages** (the grid button) shows every page as a thumbnail. A page's menu bookmarks, turns, duplicates, exports or deletes it, and you can drag pages to put them in another order.
- **Add Page** inserts new paper (the same as the page before, or another), or pages from another PDF.
- **Bookmark This Page** marks a page; the bookmarked filter in **Show Pages** lists only those.
- **Go to Page…** jumps to a page by number.

> [!tip] Paper settings
> Settings › Pencil Drawings › **Notebook Paper** sets the line spacing and the colours of new notebooks' paper.
