# Handwriting and PDFs

Graphite is built for Apple Pencil first. Handwriting goes in two places:

- **Inside a note**, as a drawing between paragraphs: a sketch, a derivation, a map of an idea. See [[Drawings in notes]].
- **On a PDF**: a notebook with paper, or slides and papers you write on. See [[Notebooks and paper]] and [[Writing on slides]].

Both use the same tools: [[Pencil tools]].

| Note | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [[Drawings in notes]] | Draw, insert, edit later; PNG, PDF or SVG |
| [[Pencil tools]] | Pens, colours arranged by dragging, the lasso, shapes, the eraser, palm rejection |
| [[Notebooks and paper]] | New notebooks, paper templates, pages, bookmarks |
| [[Writing on slides]] | Ink, highlights and text boxes on any PDF, slides in notes, sections of notes on slides |
| [[Recording a lecture]] | Audio or video while you write |
| [[PDF markup on the Mac]] | Highlight, draw, text boxes, signatures and shapes with a mouse or trackpad |

On the Mac there is no Pencil, but everything written on the iPad shows there, and PDFs have their own [[PDF markup on the Mac|markup row]].

The example course, [[Everyday Electronics]], uses all of it: slides with notes written on them, a notebook, and a drawing in the lecture notes.
