# Pencil tools

The same tools draw in notes, notebooks and PDFs. This drawing shows every one of them, made in Graphite:

![[Every Pencil tool.png]]

From the top: the eight inks (pen, monoline, fountain pen, reed pen, pencil, crayon, watercolour, highlighter); the eraser cutting through two strokes, and the lasso having moved and turned a word; the shapes (a line, an arrow, an arc, a curved arrow, a circle, an ellipse, a triangle, a square); and a picture placed on the drawing.

## A bar or a palette

Settings › Pencil Drawings › Pencil Tools › **Tools** chooses where the tools are:

- **Fixed Bar**: a row of tools above the page, in the tab bar, as Goodnotes has it.
- **Floating Palette**: Apple's palette, which you can move around the screen or make smaller.

On iPhone the palette is always used.

## The tools

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ink** | Pen, Monoline, Fountain Pen, Reed Pen, Pencil, Crayon, Watercolor, Highlighter. Tap the tool in use again to change its width, opacity or colour |
| **Eraser** | rubs out whole strokes or only the part under it |
| **Lasso** | circles strokes, text boxes and pictures to move, resize, turn, recolour, copy or delete them |
| **Text Box** | tap the page to type, or a box to edit it (PDFs) |
| **Laser Pointer** | points at something while you present, and fades |
| **Shapes** | draws clean lines, arrows, circles and rectangles |

## Your colours, arranged by dragging

The fixed bar has a row of colours, as Apple's markup has:

- tap a colour to use it; tap it again to change it in the colour picker;
- **drag a colour** along the row to put it where you want it;
- drag it up or down off the row to remove it (Undo brings it back);
- **+** at the end adds the colour in use.

## Hold to make a shape

With **Hold to Make a Shape** on, draw a line, circle, triangle or rectangle and keep the Pencil still at the end: it becomes a clean shape.

## Your hand on the page

**Palm Rejection** keeps a hand resting on the page from scrolling, zooming or tapping while Apple Pencil writes. **Draw with Finger** lets a finger draw when you have no Pencil.
