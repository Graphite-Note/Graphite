# PDF markup on the Mac

On the Mac, a PDF has a row of markup tools, so you can mark up slides and papers with a mouse or trackpad. Everything you make is a standard PDF annotation that Preview and Acrobat show, and ink drawn on the Mac stays editable with Apple Pencil on the iPad.

Show the row with View › **Show Markup Toolbar**, or the **Show Markup Toolbar** button (a pencil in a circle) in the PDF's toolbar.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Text Selection** | selects text; right-click it to mark it |
| **Highlight** | every text selection is highlighted, underlined or struck through with the chosen style when you let go, as in Preview |
| **Draw** | draws ink with the row's colour and width |
| **Eraser** | removes each whole stroke it touches |
| **Text Box** | a click places a box and starts typing; a click on a box selects it |
| **Shapes** | drag to draw a rectangle, oval, line or arrow; click a shape to move, reshape or delete it |
| **Sign** | places a signature you drew once, which Graphite keeps for next time; drag it to move or resize it |

Keyboard: ⌃⌘H highlights the selected text and ⌃⌘U underlines it.

Try it on [[Week 1 slides.pdf]].

> [!info] Where signatures are kept
> Your signatures are kept by Graphite on this Mac, outside the vault, and are never written into a note.
