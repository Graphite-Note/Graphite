# Recording a lecture

Graphite records audio or video while you take notes. The recording keeps going as you write, draw and move between notes and PDFs.

1. Tap the **microphone** button at the top right (**Record Lecture**) and choose **Record Audio** or **Record Video**. On iPhone, run **Audio recorder: Start recording** in the Command Palette.
2. Keep working. The timer at the top shows the recording.
3. Open the timer for **Pause**, **Resume**, **Stop and Save** or **Cancel**.
4. The recording is saved with the open note's attachments as an ordinary `.m4a` (audio) or `.mp4` (video) file, named after the note and the time.

To have it embedded in the note when you stop, turn on Settings › Audio Recorder › **Embed Recordings in the Current Note**. An embedded recording looks like this in the note's text, and plays where it is:

```markdown
![[Week 1 lecture 2026-11-16 10.15.m4a]]
```

> [!info] Why this guide has no recording
> This guide holds no audio or video, so nothing in it can make a sound by surprise.

> [!tip] Safe if something goes wrong
> A recording survives Graphite being closed or the device restarting: what was recorded is offered back the next time Graphite opens.
