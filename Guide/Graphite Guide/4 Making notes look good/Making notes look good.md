# Making notes look good

A note is plain text written in Markdown: a few ordinary characters around your words say how they should look. You never have to learn it all at once. Type what you know, and use the buttons for the rest:

- **On iPad and iPhone**, select words and use the bar above the keyboard: **Bold**, **Italic**, **Strikethrough**, **Highlight**, **Code**, **Heading**, lists, a task, **Indent** and **Outdent**. More is in the note's **More** menu › **Format**.
- **On the Mac**, use the **Format** menu in the note's toolbar, or right-click selected text for **Format**, **Paragraph** and **Insert**.

Every note in this part shows the Markdown in a grey box (**Copy this**), then what it becomes. Copy a box into your own notes and change the words.

> [!tip] Three ways to see a note
> **Live Preview** shows the formatting while you write, and reveals the characters only around the cursor. The **reading view** (the **pencil** button, ⌘E) shows the finished note. **Source Mode** (**More** › **View**) shows the plain text. Try all three on this note.
