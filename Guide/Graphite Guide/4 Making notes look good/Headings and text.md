# Headings and text

## Headings

**Copy this**

```markdown
# A big heading
## A section
### A smaller one
```

A `#` and a space make a heading; more `#` make smaller ones, down to six. The headings of a note make its outline: the right sidebar's **Outline** lists them, and a tap goes there.

## Emphasis

**Copy this**

```markdown
**bold**, *italic*, ***both***, ~~struck through~~,
==highlighted== and `code`
```

**What you see**

**bold**, *italic*, ***both***, ~~struck through~~, ==highlighted== and `code`

Keyboard: ⌘B bold, ⌘I italic, ⇧⌘H highlight, ⇧⌘X strikethrough, ⌃⌘1 to ⌃⌘6 headings.

## Colours

**Copy this**

```markdown
~={#e93147}Red for warnings=~, ~={#086ddd}blue for definitions=~
and ~={#08b94e}green **with bold inside**=~.
```

**What you see**

~={#e93147}Red for warnings=~, ~={#086ddd}blue for definitions=~ and ~={#08b94e}green **with bold inside**=~.

Select text and choose **Format** › **Color** to write it for you. It is the syntax of the Colors plugin for Obsidian, so Obsidian shows the colours too when that plugin is installed.

## Links to the web

**Copy this**

```markdown
[Wikipedia's page on bread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread)
```

**What you see**

[Wikipedia's page on bread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread)

Links between your own notes are simpler still: see [[Links and backlinks]].

## A line across

**Copy this**

```markdown
---
```

---

Three dashes on a line of their own draw a line across the page.
