# Lists and tasks

## Bullets and numbers

**Copy this**

```markdown
- flour
- eggs
  - two for the batter
  - one to glaze
1. Heat the oven.
2. Mix everything.
3. Bake for 25 minutes.
```

**What you see**

- flour
- eggs
  - two for the batter
  - one to glaze

1. Heat the oven.
2. Mix everything.
3. Bake for 25 minutes.

Press Return at the end of an item and the next one starts by itself. Tab moves an item in, ⇧Tab moves it out.

## Tasks

**Copy this**

```markdown
- [ ] Book the train to Lisbon
- [x] Ask Sam for the guidebook
- [ ] Read week 2 before Tuesday
```

**What you see**

- [ ] Book the train to Lisbon
- [x] Ask Sam for the guidebook
- [ ] Read week 2 before Tuesday

Tap (on the Mac, click) a box to tick it, while writing, while reading, and even inside an embedded note. With a keyboard, ⌘L ticks the task the cursor is on.

> [!tip] Find every open task
> Search for `task-todo:` to list every unticked task in the vault, or `task-todo: Lisbon` for the ones about the trip.

## Folding

A heading or a list item with something under it has a small arrow beside it. Tap it to fold away what is under it. **Fold All** and **Unfold All** are in the Command Palette.
