# Connecting notes

A folder of notes becomes much more useful once the notes point at each other. A recipe links to the restaurant where you first ate it; a trip links to the restaurants you want to try; a lecture links to its slides. Graphite then shows you those connections in several ways.

| Note | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [[Links and backlinks]] | `[[links]]` between notes, and which notes link here |
| [[Tags and properties]] | `#tags`, and facts about a note such as its rating or date |
| [[Bases]] | tables, cards and maps built from those facts |
| [[Graph and map]] | every note and its links as a picture; notes with a place on a map |
| [[Canvas]] | a board of cards you arrange and connect |
| [[Daily notes and templates]] | a note for each day, notes started from a template, and the calendar |
| [[Search]] | finding anything, with words that narrow it down |

The [[Everyday examples]] use all of these. Open [[Lisbon weekend]] and follow its links to see how they fit.
