# Canvas

A canvas is a board of cards: notes, pictures, PDFs, web links and text, arranged where you like and joined by arrows. It is an Obsidian `.canvas` file.

Open [[Course map.canvas]] for the example course laid out on a board: the course page, the two weeks, the slides and the drawing, joined by arrows that say what to read first.

![[Mac - Course map canvas.jpg|620]]

*The course map on the Mac.*

## Make one

On iPad and iPhone:

1. Choose **New** › **Canvas** and name it.
2. The bar at the bottom adds things: **Add Text Card**, **Add Note from Vault**, **Add Media from Vault**, **Add Web Link** and **Add Group**.
3. Drag a card to move it, or its handles to resize it. To connect two cards, select one and drag from the dot on its side to the other.
4. With cards selected, the bar above them colours, duplicates, deletes and arranges them. **Zoom to Fit** shows the whole board.
5. The **pencil** button switches between arranging the board and only looking at it.

On the Mac, a canvas opens to look at: drag to move around, pinch to zoom, and **Zoom to Fit** shows the whole board.
