# What Graphite adds

Graphite is a study app built around an Obsidian vault. What it adds is mostly what Obsidian's iPad app cannot do: write by hand, and work on PDFs.

## Handwriting inside notes

Put the cursor where a sketch belongs and tap **Draw**. The drawing is saved as an ordinary picture (PNG, PDF or SVG) and embedded with a normal `![[embed]]`, so Obsidian shows it as an image. In Graphite it has an **Edit** button and opens again stroke by stroke. See [[Drawings in notes]].

![[Every Pencil tool.png|420]]

## PDFs you can write on

Obsidian's PDF viewer only reads. Graphite writes ink, highlights, underlines, text boxes, pictures and bookmarks into the PDF itself, as standard annotations, and adds, moves, turns and deletes pages. Notebooks are PDFs with real paper. On the Mac, PDFs get a markup row: highlight, draw, text boxes, signatures and shapes. See [[Writing on slides]], [[Notebooks and paper]] and [[PDF markup on the Mac]].

A slide can sit in a note at the height you choose:

## Goodnotes and Notability notebooks

Notebooks from those apps come in as PDFs with editable handwriting. See [[Moving in from Goodnotes and Notability]].

## Lecture recording

The **microphone** button records audio or video while you move between notes and PDFs. See [[Recording a lecture]].

## The library

Besides Obsidian's file list, the sidebar can show your files as a **Library**: folders and page thumbnails, as the Files app shows them. See [[Library and list]].

## Native, and light on the Mac

Notes are drawn by native code, not a browser engine: Mermaid diagrams, math, tables, maps and the graph included. A web view starts only if you turn on community plugins. Several windows work on the iPad and the Mac: a PDF beside its note, or two vaults at once.

## More, briefly

- The **Map** layout of Bases is built in (Obsidian needs a plugin).
- **Export to PDF** prints a note with its formulas, drawings, diagrams and bases as the reading view shows them.
- The **Calendar** panel shows days with notes and, once you allow it, Apple Calendar's events and Reminders.
- **Read Aloud** reads notes and PDFs with a natural voice, on the device.
