# Themes and snippets

## Graphite's look

Settings › **Appearance**:

- **Base Color Scheme**: **Adapt to System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Accent Color**: a colour of your choice, a **Custom Color**, or **Use the Vault's Obsidian Accent**.
- **Font**: the interface, text and monospace fonts. **Quick Font Size Adjustment** changes the text size with a pinch over the note (on the Mac, Control-scrolling), or ⌘+ and ⌘-.
- **App Icon** (iPad and iPhone): Graphite's icon on the Home Screen.
- **Show Files As**: **List** or **Library** (see [[Library and list]]).

## Your vault's Obsidian theme

A vault can carry an Obsidian theme and CSS snippets in its `.obsidian` folder. Turn on **Use the Vault's Obsidian Theme** and notes take the theme's colours, headings, callouts and spacing. Buttons, menus and Settings keep Graphite's look. The theme is chosen in Obsidian and saved in the vault, so both apps use the same one.

## Try a snippet

A snippet is a small style file in `.obsidian/snippets`. This guide has one, *Ruled headings*, switched off:

1. Turn on **Use the Vault's Obsidian Theme**.
2. Under **CSS Snippets**, turn on **Ruled headings**.
3. Come back to this note: each second-level heading, like *Try a snippet* above, now has a line under it, and bullets are small squares.

- these

Turning a snippet on here turns it on in Obsidian too.
