# Making it yours

Open Settings: on iPad and iPhone with the **Settings** button (a gear) at the top of the sidebar, on the Mac with Graphite › **Settings…**. With a keyboard, press ⌘,.

| Note | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [[Themes and snippets]] | light and dark, your accent colour, fonts, and your vault's Obsidian theme |
| [[Plugins]] | turning features on and off, and Obsidian's community plugins |
| [[Keyboard shortcuts]] | the shortcuts, and changing them |
| [[Library and list]] | seeing your files as a list or as a library of folders and pages |
