## Paragraphs as columns

> [!multi-column]
> Each paragraph of a multi-column callout is a column of its own, as the snippet draws it in Obsidian.
>
> - A list is one column
> - with its items under each other
>
> A third paragraph wraps under the others when the note is narrower than three columns of 200 points.
