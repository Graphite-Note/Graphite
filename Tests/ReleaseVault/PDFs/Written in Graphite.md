# Written in Graphite

`Written in Graphite.pdf` is [[Lecture slides.pdf]] written on with every tool of Graphite's PDF view, by the same code the view saves with (`PDFPageManager`), in `ReleaseVaultGraphiteFilesTests`:

1. [[Written in Graphite.pdf#page=1|Slide 1]]: a wave of each ink along the foot (pen, monoline, fountain pen, reed pen, pencil, crayon, watercolor); the highlighter over the third bullet, multiplied over the text so it stays readable; a red line under the title cut twice by the pixel eraser.
2. [[Written in Graphite.pdf#page=2|Slide 2]]: text boxes in Helvetica, Georgia bold on a filled and framed box, Courier New italic and underlined, Avenir Next turned, and Menlo with a link to a web page.
3. [[Written in Graphite.pdf#page=3|Slide 3]]: a highlight, an underline, a strikeout and a green highlight over four lines, as the markup tools make them from a selection; a picture placed upright and one turned.
4. [[Written in Graphite.pdf#page=4|Slide 4]]: the shapes of the shape tool, an "ok" moved by the lasso, and a link from the exercise line to slide 1.

Its outline is the slides' own, followed by two bookmarks, "Page 2" and "Page 4", as Bookmark This Page adds them.

## The other PDFs made for the vault

- [[Dense formula sheet.pdf]]: an A5 page across, a quarter of A3, with 1,500 small handwritten strokes: as dense as the formula sheet a person imported from Goodnotes (6,000 strokes on an A3 page), on a quarter of the page so the vault stays small.
- [[Protected slides.pdf]]: the slides with an owner password and only printing allowed; Graphite opens it read-only, without asking for a password.
- [[Form.pdf]]: a form with a text field, two check boxes and a choice, which Graphite shows as they are.
- [[Odd pages.pdf]]: pages of different sizes, one turned by its `/Rotate`, one cropped.
