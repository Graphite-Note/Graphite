# Every paper and size

A notebook of each paper and each page size of the New Notebook sheet, made by its own writer (`NewNotebookDocument`) in `ReleaseVaultGraphiteFilesTests`.

## Papers

On A5, two pages each. The covers take each of the eight colours in turn, the lines each colour (gray, blue, green, red) and strength (light, standard, strong), and the paper each colour (white, ivory, yellow, gray); the last, Music Paper, has no cover.
