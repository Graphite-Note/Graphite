# Every tool

`Every tool.png` is a drawing made with every tool of Graphite's Pencil bar, saved the three ways Settings › Pencil Drawings › Save Drawings As offers: [[Every tool.png|PNG]], [[Every tool.svg|SVG]] and [[Every tool.pdf|PDF]]. Each carries its strokes, so it opens for editing. `ReleaseVaultGraphiteFilesTests` makes it from points, as no Pencil draws in a test.

![[Every tool.png]]

From the top:

1. **Inks**: a wave in pen, monoline, fountain pen, reed pen, pencil, crayon and watercolor, and the highlighter (PencilKit's marker) last.
2. **Eraser and lasso**: a black line and a pink watercolor band, each cut through by the pixel eraser, so the line is three pieces and the band two; on the right a small handwritten "ok" moved to the right and turned a little by the lasso.
3. **Shapes**: what the shape tool makes of a stroke: a line, an arrow, an arc, a curved arrow, a circle, a turned ellipse, a triangle and a rectangle.
4. **A picture** placed on the drawing, turned a little.

[[Without paper.png]] is the same strokes with a clear background, and no picture.

The same tools on a PDF page are in [[Written in Graphite]].
