Created: 2026-11-26.

Status: 2026-11-26, built, tested and checked on the iPad and iPhone simulators and the Mac. Every item is ticked with what checked it and what was not checked. Issues: [#256](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/256) to [#259](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/259), one per section, and [#268](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/268) for section 5, built 2026-11-28, and [#274](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/274) for section 6, built 2026-11-28, and [#278](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/278) for section 7, built 2026-11-28.

## Request

Feature 9 of [Notion-features-for-Graphite.md](Notion-features-for-Graphite.md): many students choose Notion for how a course page looks, with an icon on every page, a cover picture and blocks side by side. Graphite keeps that look with properties and callouts that Obsidian already reads, without a format of its own:

- An `icon` property (an emoji or an SF Symbol name) shown in tabs, the file list, the quick switcher, links and Library mode, set from the note's menu.
- A `banner` property drawn as a short cover in reading view only, collapsible, and off on the iPhone by default, so it does not cost the screen space the editor needs.
- `> [!multi-column]` callouts drawn as columns where the note is wide enough and stacked when it is narrow. The file stays a callout that every Markdown reader shows.

Every extra must cost nothing for notes without these properties.

## What the result must be

- **The same file in Obsidian.** The properties and the callout are the ones the most used plugins and snippets write, so a note looks alike in Obsidian with those plugins (decisions 1 to 3). Graphite writes nothing else.
- **Nothing extra for notes without them.** A vault without an `icon` property costs one indexed query that returns no rows after each index change. A note without `banner` and without these callouts builds and draws as before. Measured in section 4.
- **No chrome above the page.** The cover is drawn inside the reading view's scrolling content, above the title, never as a fixed row, and never in Live Preview or source mode.
- **No inert controls.** An icon Graphite cannot draw (an Iconize pack other than Lucide, a Lucide icon without an SF Symbol here) draws nothing, and the property stays as written.

## Decisions

1. **`icon` is Iconize's frontmatter field.** Iconize reads the `icon` property when its "Use frontmatter" option is on (off by default, `iconInFrontmatterEnabled`, `iconInFrontmatterFieldName: 'icon'` in its `src/settings/data.ts`). Pixel Banner also draws `icon` on its banner (`customBannerIconField: ['icon']` in its `src/settings/settings.js`). Iconize accepts an emoji or an icon pack name with its prefix (`IbBell`, `LiBookOpen`). Graphite reads an emoji (the first one of the text, as Iconize does), a Lucide name of Iconize's built-in pack (`Li` and the Lucide name in upper camel case, drawn from Obsidian's own Lucide set without a download), and an SF Symbol name. Other packs (Font Awesome, Remix and so on) are downloads of Iconize that Graphite does not have: they draw nothing.
2. **A symbol chosen in Graphite is written as its Lucide name.** The picker offers 64 symbols, each an SF Symbol paired with the Lucide icon that shows the same thing (`NoteIconSymbols`), and writes the Lucide name (`LiBookOpen`), so Obsidian with Iconize shows the same icon. All 64 Lucide names were checked against the icon table in Obsidian 1.15.1. An SF Symbol name typed by hand is read too, and Obsidian shows nothing for it.
3. **`banner` is the field Banners and Pixel Banner share.** Banners (noatpad/obsidian-banners README) writes `banner` with `banner_x` and `banner_y` from 0 to 1. Pixel Banner (jparkerweb/pixel-banner README and settings) writes `banner` with `banner-y` as a percentage, and reads a wikilink, an embed, a Markdown link or embed, a plain path, a web address or search words. Graphite reads every form except Pixel Banner's search words (its online service) and `file://` paths outside the vault, and takes the vertical position from `banner_y` or `banner-y`. The horizontal position, height, fade and the other options of both plugins are not read: Graphite's cover has one short height for every note.
4. **Columns follow Modular CSS Layout.** The `[!multi-column]` callout is the snippet `MCL Multi Column.css` of efemkay/obsidian-modular-css-layout: the callout's title and box are hidden, each block of its body (a nested callout, a paragraph, a list, a quote) is a column at least 200 px wide that grows to share the width (`flex: 1 1 200px`, gap 1em), and columns wrap onto a new row when the width runs out. Graphite draws the same rule at 200 points (`CalloutColumnArrangement`), so a phone shows one column per row. `[!blank]` and `[!blank-container]` from the same snippet draw their content without title or box.
5. **Callout metadata is read.** Obsidian reads `> [!type|metadata]`, which the snippet uses for column widths (`[!note|wide-2]`). Graphite did not recognise such a line as a callout at all. The header now takes the metadata in the reading view, Live Preview, Read Aloud's folds and the plugin HTML (`data-callout-metadata`).
6. **One switch.** Icons, covers and columns are one core plugin, "Icons, Covers and Columns", on by default. Off, nothing is queried or drawn and the callouts show as ordinary callouts, as Obsidian shows them without the snippet.
7. **A cover is collapsed per note for the session**, as a callout's folding is (`CalloutFoldStore`). The note's file is never changed by collapsing it.
8. **Covers on the iPhone are a setting, off by default.** The phone's reading view starts with the title.

## 5. Pages imported from Notion look as they do in Notion

Issue: [#268](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/268)

Requested 2026-11-27, after a real Notion workspace was imported and compared with Notion itself: the importer writes Notion's page icons as pictures (`icon: "[[clock.gif]]"`, or a web address of Notion's own icon set such as `icon: https://app.notion.com/icons/clock_gray.svg`, about 550 pages of one workspace), covers as `banner`, and pages as headings over lines that each link to a page. Iconize does not draw a picture icon (it reads emoji and icon pack names), so in Obsidian these show only with Graphite's reading of them. Notion draws a page's icon large, over the lower left of its cover, the cover across the whole width above the title, and a link to a page as its small icon and title.

Decisions:

9. **A picture icon is the third kind of icon.** `NoteIcon.picture` reads every form `banner` takes (a wikilink or embed, a Markdown link, a plain path, a web address), so a picture of the vault or of the web, an SVG included, which Graphite draws with its own SVG renderer. Web pictures are fetched when first shown, at most 2 MB, and kept in memory for the window, never written to the vault.
10. **The cover spans the reading view, as Notion's.** It is drawn across the whole width of the reading view, outside the readable column, 30% of the view's height between 140 and 280 points, and the page's icon sits over its lower left. A GIF shows its first frame.
11. **A run of lines that each link to a note is a compact list**, one row per link, its icon then its title, as Notion's links to pages. The note's text stays as written.
12. **Spacing is proposed, not changed here.** Graphite's spacing between blocks is measured against Notion's at the same width and the differences are recorded below with numbers, for a decision that applies to every note.

## 6. Colours and column widths of pages imported from Notion

Issue: [#274](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/274)

Requested 2026-11-28 for the same imported workspaces: the importer writes Notion's coloured text and coloured backgrounds as inline HTML, `<span style="color:#rrggbb">…</span>` and `background-color`, which Obsidian draws natively, and Notion's uneven columns as `> > [!blank|wide-N]` inside `> [!multi-column]`, Modular CSS Layout's `wide-2` to `wide-5`.

Decisions:

13. **A span's colours are drawn whatever the Colors core plugin says**, since they are HTML that Obsidian draws without a plugin. `HTMLColorSpans` (Core) reads `color`, `background-color` and `background` written as `#rgb`, `#rrggbb`, `#rrggbbaa`, `rgb()` or `rgba()`, and a span inside code stays text.
14. **Coloured text stays readable in light and dark.** Text drawn in a colour of the note, a span's or the Colors plugin's, is darkened on a light page or lightened on a dark one by the least amount that reaches a 4.5:1 contrast (`AccentLegibility`), as Graphite's code colours already are, and a coloured background is drawn as written on a light page and as a dark tint of its hue on a dark one (22% lightness, as Notion's own dark backgrounds are 19 to 25%), since Notion's pale backgrounds would otherwise put light text on a light patch. The note keeps the colour as written.
15. **The colour command knows spans.** With the cursor or selection in a span's coloured text, choosing a colour rewrites the span's `color` and Remove takes the span's colours out, keeping the text and any other style of the span.
16. **Column widths follow the snippet.** A nested callout's `wide-2` to `wide-5` makes its column grow two to five times as much as the others, as the snippet's `flex-grow` does, so `CalloutColumnArrangement`'s weights now come from each column's metadata.

## 7. The page's header and its links on a real imported workspace

Issue: [#278](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/278)

Requested 2026-11-28 after the wiki home page of the first re-import of a real workspace was compared with the live Notion page: no icon before any link of the page, the title written with `.md`, and the Properties panel showing the `banner` and `icon` rows that the cover and the large icon already draw. The colour spans of that page were drawn raw in the build compared, made before section 6.

Decisions:

17. **The inline title is the note's name without `.md`**, as Obsidian's inline title and Notion's page title are, whatever Settings › Files and Links › Show File Extensions says. The tab and the file list keep that setting.
18. **The reading view leaves the cover's and the icon's rows to the page's header.** While the plugin draws them, `banner`, `banner_y`, `banner-y` and `icon` are not shown in the Properties panel, and a note with no other property shows no panel, as Notion shows none. They stay in the note and in every edit of the panel. A banner that names no picture keeps its row. Live Preview and source mode show every row.
19. **A read of the icons is never cut short.** The window's icon catalog reads the icons again after each change of the index. Each change cancelled the read under way, so while a large vault's first index was written no read finished and no link had its icon. One read now runs at a time, and the newest request is answered by a read that starts after it.

- [x] The catalog's reads run to their end while the index changes (decision 19), and a picture link that cannot be read drops only its own icon. Done 2026-11-28: `NoteIconCatalog.requestReload`, called by `PageLookLayer`, and `VaultIndex.resolvingPictureLinks`. Tests: `ReadingCoverTests.testAReloadAskedForDuringARunningReadIsAnsweredAfterItInsteadOfCancellingIt`. The comparison app's own index was looked up read-only and holds the icons (2,592 rows), so a read that never finished is the likely cause there. Not checked in that app on that vault.
- [x] The inline title without `.md` (decision 17). Done 2026-11-28: `MarkdownPane.inlineTitle` writes the note's stem. Checked in the pictures below with Show File Extensions on, as by default.
- [x] The cover's and the icon's rows left out of the reading view's Properties panel (decision 18). Done 2026-11-28: `ReadingPageLook.drawnPropertyKeys`, `RenderedBlock.properties(_:hiddenKeys:)`, `PropertiesPanel.hiddenKeys`, `NoteBanner.coverPropertyKeys`. Tests: `ReadingCoverTests.testTheReadingViewLeavesTheCoversAndIconsRowsToThePagesHeader` (rows kept in the panel's edits, no panel for a note with only a cover, a cover not drawn keeps its row).
- [x] Checked with a made-up page of the wiki home's shape (pages in a subfolder, `> > ---` opening each column, a hard break after each link, `[[path|alias]]` links, emoji and picture icons) on the Mac and the iPad simulator. Done 2026-11-28: drawn in the Mac app's window from a test that was not kept (every icon before its link, the title without `.md`, no Properties panel), and hosted `NotionLookHostedTests` on an iPad Pro 11-inch (M5) simulator (iOS 27.2), with the plugin on and off. Not checked: a physical device.
- [x] A plain page before links without an icon in a list of links where another link has one, so the list's text lines up as in Notion. Requested 2026-11-28 after the comparison confirmed decision 19: once the index settled, every icon of the wiki home drew. Done 2026-11-28: `ReadingViewBuilder.linksWithoutIconInIconLists` finds the links alone on a line of a list of two or more such lines (`CompactLinkLines.isLinkLine`, hard breaks or paragraphs drawn as one list) and `pageGlyphPrefix` draws SF Symbol `doc.text` in grey, as symbol icons are. Links in prose, and lists where no link has an icon, are left as they are. Tests: `ReadingCoverTests.testLinksWithoutAnIconInAListOfIconLinksGetAPageGlyphAndProseLinksDoNot`, the made-up course hub's pictures on the Mac and the iPad simulator. Emoji are a little wider than the 1.1 em pictures, so a picture's text starts about 4 points left of an emoji's.
- [x] Icons read for a vault opened unchanged since its last scan. Reported 2026-11-28 on the fourth re-import: no note's icon showed outside its own page (links, tabs), 4 minutes after launch with the index settled. The window's catalog read the icons again when the vault or the index's properties changed, but the index arrives after the vault's identity, so the one read made in between found no index, and a vault unchanged since its last scan changes no property to read again. Not a regression of decision 19: the same made-up vault, opened unchanged, showed no icon on 63696792 either. Earlier comparisons opened a vault the importer had just rewritten, whose scan changed properties. Done 2026-11-28: `WorkspaceModel.noteIconSource`'s key holds the index's identity. Tests: `NoteIconCatalogLoadMacTests.testAVaultUnchangedSinceItsLastScanStillShowsItsIcons` (a window's page-look layer over a vault opened again unchanged, which timed out without the change), and a made-up vault of 3,013 notes drawn in the Mac app's window fresh, rewritten and unchanged, with icons in each after the change.
- [x] Every icon before a link in the same square, as Notion gives each icon a 20-point box. Compared on the fourth re-import's wiki home: lines with an emoji or a picture were taller than lines with the page glyph, the glyph sat lower than an emoji and its text started about 4 points further left, and small dark pictures looked smaller than emoji. Done 2026-11-28: an emoji icon is drawn as a picture too (`NoteIconSquarePicture.emoji`, Core Text), a vault or web picture is fitted into a square (`NoteIconSquarePicture.squared`), and every icon is a square of 1.25 em, Notion's 20 points beside 16-point text, 18% of it below the baseline as an emoji's own place (`NoteIconInlineAddress`, `VaultImageAttachment.iconSide`). The emoji stays the picture's description. Tests: `ReadingCoverTests.testEveryIconBeforeALinkIsASquareOfTheSameSizeOnTheSameBaseline` and the icon tests before it, the made-up course hub's pictures on the Mac (rows 25 points apart, every text at the same place) and the iPad simulator, light and dark.
- [x] A long link to a page kept on one line, as Notion cuts it with an ellipsis in a narrow column. Compared on the fourth re-import's wiki home after the icon squares: a long link in a narrow column wrapped, and its second line started under the icon at the column's left edge. Done 2026-11-28: a paragraph whose every line is an icon and one link (`ReadingIconLinkLines`, not a list item, a table or a line with other text) is drawn one text per line, each limited to one line and cut at its end (Textual's `TruncatedLinesAttribute`, patch 38). Lines that fit take the same height as before, each within a pixel of its place. Tests: `ReadingCoverTests.testLinesOfAnIconAndALinkStayOnOneLineAndOtherTextWraps` and `testAMarkedParagraphKeepsEachLineOnOneLineAndDrawsShortLinesAsOneTextDoes` (the same height, and a long line cut at 120 points where an unmarked one wraps), and the made-up course hub's pictures on the Mac, with a long link in its Labs column. The full title is not shown on hover; it is read aloud, copied and searched whole.
- [x] Inline code on a rounded chip in the reading view, as Notion and Obsidian draw it. Compared on the fifth re-import: inline code was bare monospace text with no background, which hardly stands apart from the prose. Done 2026-11-28: `ObsidianMarkdownParser.drawInlineCodeAsChips` puts each code span on a rounded background (the theme's code background, or Live Preview's tint without a theme, Obsidian's 4-pixel corners and 2 points beyond its text at 16-point text, both scaled with the text size), and kerns the character before and the one after it by those 2 points, so the chip keeps a whole space from the text beside it. A highlight around code keeps its own colour. The colour of the code's text stays the note's, as in Obsidian, not Notion's red. Tests: `InlineCodeChipTests` (3: the chip and its sizes, the kerned spaces, a theme's colour, a highlight kept, the chip's pixels drawn), and the made-up course hub's pictures with a line of inline code on the Mac and the iPad simulator, light and dark.
- [x] A Notion callout drawn as Notion draws it: the importer writes one as a callout titled with its icon alone (`> [!tip] 💡`, then the body). Requested 2026-11-28 from the comparison's demo pages. Done 2026-11-28: a callout whose title is a single emoji (`CalloutIconTitle.emoji(in:)`: an emoji drawn as one by default, or asked to be with U+FE0F, a keycap, a modifier or a joined sequence, never a digit or `#`) is drawn by `CalloutView`, in the reading view and Live Preview, with the emoji on the first line of its body (1.35 times the text size, Notion's 22 pixels at 16), no title row and no type icon, in the type's tint. A foldable one (`[!tip]- 💡`) keeps its title row to fold by. Obsidian shows a title row with the emoji. A Notion icon that is one of Notion's own pictures has no emoji, so the importer writes `> [!info|no-title]` over the body (the word some Obsidian themes use to hide a title, chosen over hiding every empty title, which would change native callouts): drawn the same way with the type's glyph, only with `no-title` in the metadata, a body, and no fold (`CalloutIconTitle.hidesTitle`, `NotePreviewDocument` gives it an empty title). A native `> [!info]` keeps its "Info" row. Every icon is as wide as the emoji's size, so the text of these callouts starts at the same place. Tests: `CalloutIconTitleTests` (4, Core: the emoji, the metadata word among others, the title hidden only over a body and without a fold), `CalloutNotionIconTests` (3: no title row with an emoji or a hidden title, a foldable one and other titles keep theirs), and the made-up coloured page's pictures with a tip, a warning and a `no-title` info callout in both views on the Mac and the iPad simulator.
