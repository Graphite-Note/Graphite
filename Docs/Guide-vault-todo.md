## Request

Request of 2026-11-16: "using the test folder vault we did a proper guide. The current guide is, you know, nothing. I want like a detailed guide, like what the app is actually capable of, for someone that does know Obsidian and for someone that doesn't know anything. Like, let's say someone is used to like good notes, and you're like want them to go into this open file system, you know, local on your computer or on a drive, or, you know, explain that to them. And then what the notes are capable of, from like, you know, the handwriting to how can you make the markdown look nice, etc. Get inspired from my vault if you want, but do not post my personal stuff. But, you know, from things like, hey, you can put your favorite restaurants or your recipes or your notes, cross-reference things. You can actually like share like single folders on using iCloud Drive or any other drive you choose. Small things of that nature. You can like make the guide visual, and, you know, how can you show the syntax and whatever."

Today the Guide is a list of short how-to topics inside the app (`GuideContent`, `GuideCopy*.swift`): steps in text, a few syntax examples, no pictures, nothing to try. `Tests/ReleaseVault` (the release test vault) has a file for every feature, written to be checked, not read.

## Decisions

- The guide is itself a vault, **Graphite Guide**: notes that show each feature working, beside the syntax that makes it, so a reader sees the result and can open the source. Graphite opens a copy of it that the reader can change freely; the original stays in the app.
- Two ways in: one path for someone coming from Goodnotes or Notability, who has never kept notes as files; one for someone who knows Obsidian, which says what Graphite adds and what differs. Both meet in the same feature notes.
- Made-up content only: example recipes, restaurants, a course, a trip, a reading list, in the spirit of the real vault but none of its text, names or pictures (`no-personal-notes-in-demos`). The release test vault's generators and files are reused where they fit.
- How it reaches the app (delegated 2026-11-16, simplest option under the lean-core rule): the vault is a download-only add-on pack (`GuideVaultPack`), every file of `Guide/Graphite Guide` at a pinned commit, fetched the first time the Guide opens it and listed in Settings › General › Add-ons. Nothing is added to the app's bundle. The Guide opens the vault through a `graphite://guide?file=…` link, so the sheet and the Mac's Guide window work the same way.
- The reader's copy (delegated 2026-11-16): made once in Graphite's own Documents folder (On My iPad › Graphite in Files) and opened again after that; **Make a Fresh Copy** puts a new copy beside the old one ("Graphite Guide 2") and never deletes what the reader wrote.
- Layout (delegated 2026-11-16): seven numbered folders that follow this file's sections (1 New to files, 2 Coming from Obsidian, 3 Handwriting and PDFs, 4 Making notes look good, 5 Connecting notes, 6 Everyday examples, 7 Making it yours), plus `Start here`, `Templates` and `Attachments`. The examples are a made-up evening course (*Everyday Electronics*), recipes, made-up restaurants placed in Lisbon, a Lisbon weekend and a reading list of made-up books; pictures are flat drawings made by `scripts/make-guide-vault.py`, plus a drawing and a notebook that Graphite's own writers made for the release vault.
- Visual: screenshots of Graphite on the iPad and the Mac, taken in simulators and a test copy of the Mac app from the guide vault itself, light and dark; small JPEGs. The whole vault stays small (a target of 5 MB).

## 7. Guide updates reach the reader's copy, and moving in from Notion

Issue: [#289](https://github.com/Graphite-Note/Graphite/issues/289)

Reported 2026-11-28: "i didnt see the notion stuff in the guide vault". The TestFlight builds since 14:10 pin the guide with the dashboards of section 6, but the Guide reopens the copy it made the first time (`GuideVaultCopy`), and only **Make a Fresh Copy** brought new notes, beside the old copy. The pack itself is fetched again when the app's pinned commit changes (`AddOnPackStore.isInstalled` compares the revision), so the newer files were on the device and never reached the copy. The guide also had no page about moving in from Notion.

Decisions:

- When the Guide opens a copy made from an earlier pack, the copy is brought up to the pack before it opens. A guide file the reader never changed (its SHA-256 is the one the pack has now or one an earlier pack had for that path) is replaced by the new version; a file new in the pack is added; a file the earlier pack had and the new one does not is removed if unchanged. A note the reader changed is kept as it is, and the Guide's new version goes beside it as "Name (Guide update).md". The reader's own files, and settings the reader changed in `.obsidian`, are left alone. A note the reader deleted is not brought back.
- The hashes of earlier packs are the files of every revision `GuideVaultPack` was pinned at, written beside the current list by `scripts/make-guide-vault.py --pack-revision` (only the pairs of a path and a hash the current pack does not have). The copy's revision and its pack's file names are app-side settings (`GuideVaultCopy`), as the copy's name already is: nothing is added to the vault.
- After an update the Guide says once what changed, in the notice over the note: the new pages, how many pages were updated, and the notes whose changes were kept, with the name of the new version beside each.
