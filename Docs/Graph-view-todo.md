Created: 2026-10-21.

Priority: high. The owner asked for it on 2026-10-21, looking at his vault's graph on the Mac: "improve drastically, make it more accessible, not through view then graph view, make tags available and make it just look nicer".

Status: 2026-11-12 on the branch `graph-view-way-in-and-size`: the iPad's graph button one tap from the file list (section 1), section 4 measured and the graph made ready, laid out and settled off the main thread on several cores, the ring of notes without links made thin, and the hosted tests of section 5. Section 3 on the branch `graph-view-look` (2026-10-22): Obsidian's groups kept per vault on the device, Names From Zoom, Node Size and Link Thickness, names that fade in and never overlap, a halo on the open note, colors for light and dark chosen against the accent, and a first drawing that is calm and comes back where it was left. Sections 1 and 2 on the branch `graph-view-ways-in` (2026-10-22). Section 2: tags shown by default with a # toggle over the graph, chosen with a tap to bring out their notes, named from further out, counted in the corner; nested tags stay the written tags, as in Obsidian. Section 1, all but the iPad's one-tap button: the graph opens as a tab, notes open beside it, a button in the file list, ⇧⌘G and ⌥⌘G, the local graph from a note's More menu and back to the whole graph centered on the note, Show in Graph on tags and folders, and the Guide. Before that: the graph itself ships and is correct. `LinkGraph`, `GraphLinkResolver` and `GraphLayout` build it, `VaultGraphView` (then `VaultGraphScreen`), `LocalGraphPanel` and `GraphCanvas` draw it, `LinkGraphTests`, `VaultGraphTests` and `GraphLinkResolverTests` cover it, and it was checked on the iPad on 2026-09-24 (`Coverage.md`). Nothing in this file is built yet: it is about reaching the graph, tags in it, how it looks, and how it behaves on a vault the owner's size.

## Request

Today the whole-vault graph is reached through View › Graph View on the Mac or "Graph view: Open graph view" in the command palette, and it opens as a modal: a page-sized sheet on the Mac, a full-screen cover on iPadOS (`GraphiteRootView`, `workspace.isGraphPresented`). It closes as soon as a note is opened from it. Tags are off by default and their toggle sits inside the Graph Settings popover. The drawing is plain: gray notes, links at 18 percent, names only from a zoom of 1.4, no colored groups.

The owner wants it one tap away, tags there without hunting for them, and a graph that is good to look at on a vault of about 4,700 notes.

## Decisions

- It stays Obsidian's graph: the same links, filters and forces, read from the same index. This file is about the way in, tags and the look, not a second graph.
- Its settings stay on the device (`GraphSettingKey` in `WorkspaceGraph.swift`), as Obsidian keeps them in its workspace. Nothing new is written into the vault.
- Groups are Obsidian's groups: a search and a color, not a new file format.
- The look follows Graphite's theme and accent colors, not Obsidian's palette.
